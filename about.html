Our Story

Cherry Pop opened its doors in 1958, tucked into the corner of a small town, with nothing but a soda fountain, four stools, and a big dream: to make the best sundae in town.

What started as a tiny family counter soon became the place where everyone showed up — first dates, birthday scoops, and Sunday afternoons that stretched a little too long. The recipes never changed much. The love in them never did either.

Today, Cherry Pop keeps that same spirit alive — one scoop, one splash of syrup, one cherry on top at a time.
